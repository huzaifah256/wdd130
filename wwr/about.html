About This Course

WDD130 - Web Fundamentals is an introduction to web development covering HTML structure, CSS styling, responsive design principles, and best practices for creating accessible websites. Through hands-on projects, I'm learning to build professional web pages that meet modern web standards.

Uganda

Uganda, known as the "Pearl of Africa," is a beautiful landlocked country in East Africa. Famous for its diverse wildlife, including mountain gorillas, and the source of the Nile River, Uganda offers stunning landscapes from the Rwenzori Mountains to Lake Victoria. The country is rich in culture with over 50 different ethnic groups and languages, making it a vibrant and diverse nation.
